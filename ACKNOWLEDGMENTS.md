# Acknowledgments

This project builds on Craig S. Kaplan's `hatviz`, a p5.js visualizer for
constructing aperiodic hat tilings. The tiling construction and original
browser-visualization foundation are due to that work.

The underlying hat monotile comes from the work of David Smith, Joseph Samuel
Myers, Craig S. Kaplan, and Chaim Goodman-Strauss on aperiodic monotiles.

This repository adds tools for studying tiling billiards on these aperiodic
tilings, including trajectory tracing, diagnostics,
symbolic cutting sequences, and batch experiments.

The project also bundles `p5.min.js`, the p5.js graphics library, which is used
for browser drawing and UI controls. p5.js is maintained by the Processing
Foundation and contributors.

The mathematical direction of this project was motivated by the apparent lack
of prior work on billiard dynamics in aperiodic tilings, aside from brief
mention that Diana Davis and Pat Hooper had considered related simulations for Penrose tiling
billiards.
